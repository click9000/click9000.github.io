13:8 Orbits — Venus & Earth Flower

Venus goes around the Sun 13 times in the same span that Earth goes around 8 times. If you draw a line between the two planets every few days and keep every line, the lines slowly weave themselves into a five-petaled flower — because 13 − 8 = 5, Venus overtakes Earth exactly five times per cycle. After exactly 8 years, the pattern closes almost perfectly.

The interactive page below recreates that dance, inspired by the YouTube short of the same name. Watch the flower bloom, pause it, and adjust the speed and trace fade — or switch to true orbital periods and see the flower drift instead of closing.
